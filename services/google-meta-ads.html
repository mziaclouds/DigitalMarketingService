Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum ad spend?

We recommend a minimum monthly budget of $1,000 for Google Ads and $500 for Facebook/Instagram Ads to see meaningful results. However, we can work with any budget to maximize your returns.

How do you target my ads?

We use a combination of audience targeting including demographics, interests, behaviors, location, and custom audiences. For Google Ads, we use keyword targeting and remarketing. For Meta, we create lookalike audiences and custom audiences from your data.

Do you manage all ad platforms?

Yes, we manage campaigns across Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, Video), Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram), and we can also manage TikTok, LinkedIn, and Pinterest ads on request.
